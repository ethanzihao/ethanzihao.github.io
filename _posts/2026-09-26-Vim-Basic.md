# Basic commands

## insert model 

`i`
## normal model

`esc`
## Save and exit

1. save and exit: press `esc` to enter normal model, then type `:wq` 
2. exit without saving: `esc` , then `:q!` 
3. save: `esc` , then `:w` 
## move cursor

`h j k l` ——> `left down up right` 
`A` ——> move to the end of current line and enter insert model
`$` ——> move to the rightmost word of current  line (in vim, cursor represents a word rather than a gap between two words, so you cannot put a cursor at the end of a line when in normal model)
